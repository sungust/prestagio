A great stay is not the same as a luxurious one. The most memorable hotels are the ones that belong to their place, with a room that frames the view you came for and a location that makes your days easier. These are the questions we ask.

## What we look for

- **Place.** Does the hotel make the most of where it is? Think of a villa on the water, a palazzo on the canal, a house on the Atlantic shore.
- **Position.** Does its location suit the journey? A hotel near the ferries, the passes or the old town saves hours.
- **Arrival.** How do you get there, and is the transfer part of the pleasure?
- **Rooms.** Are the best rooms worth the difference? Which ones should you ask for?
- **Season.** When is it open, and when is it at its best?

## How we work

Our stay recommendations draw on research: official sources, the properties' own published information and our editors' knowledge. Where an article is based on research rather than a visit, we say so. We do not publish invented reviews, ratings, prices or availability, and we do not describe visits we have not made.

## Affiliate links

Some stays include a link to book through a partner such as Agoda. If you book through one of these links, Prestagio may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Partner links do not decide what we recommend, and every recommendation is useful without one. Prestagio does not take bookings and cannot guarantee rates. Please read our [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure).

## Corrections

Hotels change: they renovate, change hands and close for the season. If you find something out of date, please [tell us](/contact?topic=correction). We correct factual errors promptly and note significant corrections on the article.
